Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Jme's Helpdesk LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles information in connection with our applications and services, including our use of the Instagram Graph API.

Information We Collect

Our application accesses Instagram account data through the Meta/Instagram Graph API solely for the purpose of managing and publishing content to accounts that have explicitly authorized our application. This may include:

  • Instagram account profile information (username, follower count)
  • Post metrics (likes, comments, reach, impressions)
  • Media and caption content for publishing

How We Use Information

Information accessed through the Instagram API is used only to:

  • Publish content to authorized Instagram accounts
  • Retrieve performance metrics for accounts we manage
  • Support internal reporting and content strategy

We do not sell, share, or transfer Instagram data to third parties. We do not use Instagram data for advertising or user profiling.

Data Retention

We do not store Instagram user data beyond what is required for the immediate operation of our tools. Access tokens are stored securely in private configuration files and are never shared publicly.

User Rights

You may revoke our application's access to your Instagram account at any time through your Instagram account settings under "Apps and Websites."

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect access credentials and data. All API access is conducted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
